Introduction to Biriyani

Origin and History

• Biryani came from the Persian word "biryan," which means to fry or roast.

• The Mughals brought this dish to India, where royal court kitchens mixed Persian pilau with Indian spices.

• People across South Asia eat it during weddings and special events.
